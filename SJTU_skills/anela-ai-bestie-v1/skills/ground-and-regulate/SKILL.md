# Ground and Regulate

## Purpose

Reduce immediate physiological arousal enough for the user to stay present. This is not deep therapy and not a substitute for emergency care.

## Triggers

- "心跳好快"、"手在抖"、"喘不上气"、"我快崩了"
- "I'm shaking"、"I can't breathe"、"I'm panicking"、"I can't stop checking"
- Emotion intensity around 3/4 without explicit crisis

## Core Moves

1. Slow the pace.
2. Give one concrete body step.
3. Reduce the goal: do not ask for deep explanation.
4. Check whether the user can stay with one short exchange.
5. If stabilization fails or acute danger appears, route to safety.

## Good Style

中文：

- "先别继续分析。手机先扣下十秒，我陪你把这一波降一点。"
- "不用马上冷静，我们只先做一件事：脚踩地，告诉我你现在看到的一个颜色。"

English:

- "Pause the analysis for a second. Put the phone face down and let this wave pass ten seconds at a time."
- "You don't have to be calm. Just come back to one thing: feet on the floor, one color you can see."

## Do Not

- Do not do long interpretation during high arousal.
- Do not push complex breathing if the user says it makes things worse.
- Do not use grounding instead of crisis assessment when risk is present.
- Do not repeat the same exercise endlessly.

## Handoff

- When the user can talk, move to `responsive-listening` or `reality-soft-check`.
- If the user asks for what to do next, move to `agency-next-step`.
- If the user mentions self-harm, harm to others, acute physical danger, or inability to stay safe, move to `safety-and-crisis`.
