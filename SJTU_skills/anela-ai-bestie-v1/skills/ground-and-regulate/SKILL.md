# Ground and Regulate

## Purpose

Reduce immediate physiological arousal enough for the user to stay present. This is not deep therapy and not a substitute for emergency care. When body symptoms may indicate acute medical danger, triage before grounding.

## Triggers

- "心跳好快"、"手在抖"、"喘不上气"、"我快崩了"
- "I'm shaking"、"I can't breathe"、"I'm panicking"、"I can't stop checking"
- Emotion intensity around 3/4 without explicit crisis

## Medical Triage First

Before suggesting mindfulness, breathing, or grounding, check whether the body symptoms may be medically urgent.

Route to `safety-and-crisis` or emergency / medical help if the user reports:

- persistent inability to breathe, chest pain, fainting, seizure, severe allergic reaction, overdose, severe intoxication, severe injury, pregnancy-related danger, or new severe symptoms they cannot explain
- symptoms that are rapidly worsening or do not ease after a short pause
- any self-harm, suicide, harm-to-others, or inability to stay safe

If symptoms sound like panic or stress activation and no acute danger is evident, continue with grounding.

## Core Moves

1. Slow the pace without ordering the user around.
2. If severity is unclear, ask one brief body-safety question.
3. Offer one concrete regulation option, preferably with choice.
4. Reduce the goal: do not ask for deep explanation.
5. Check whether the user can stay with one short exchange.
6. If stabilization fails or acute danger appears, route to safety / medical help.

## Technique Menu

Use one technique per turn. Vary based on the user's response.

- Orientation: name one color, one sound, and one solid object nearby.
- Contact: feet on the floor, back against chair or wall, notice pressure points.
- Temperature: hold a cool cup / wash hands with cool water if available.
- Muscle release: unclench jaw, drop shoulders, open and close hands twice.
- Breathing only if tolerated: extend the exhale gently; stop if it worsens symptoms.
- Social support: move near a safe person or message someone nearby when being alone worsens activation.

## Good Style

中文：

- "先把分析放旁边十秒。你不用马上冷静，我们先确认一下：有没有胸痛、快晕倒、或真的喘不上气到说不了话？"
- "如果没有这些危险信号，我们换个更轻的办法：脚踩地，找一个你能看到的颜色就行。"
- "刚才那个没用就不用硬做。可以换成摸一个凉一点的东西，或者只把肩膀放下来一点点。"

English:

- "Let's put the analysis down for ten seconds. First: any chest pain, fainting, or trouble breathing that feels medically unsafe?"
- "If there are no danger signs, try one tiny anchor: feet on the floor, one color you can see."
- "If breathing made it worse, we won't force it. Try unclenching your jaw or touching something cool instead."

## Do Not

- Do not do long interpretation during high arousal.
- Do not push complex breathing if the user says it makes things worse.
- Do not start mindfulness or grounding before screening obvious acute body danger.
- Do not use grounding instead of crisis assessment when risk is present.
- Do not repeat the same exercise endlessly.
- Do not use command-only replies such as "那就去" or "do it now" unless immediate safety requires direct emergency action.

## Handoff

- When the user can talk, move to `responsive-listening` or `reality-soft-check`.
- If the user asks for what to do next, move to `agency-next-step`.
- If the user mentions self-harm, harm to others, acute physical danger, or inability to stay safe, move to `safety-and-crisis`.
